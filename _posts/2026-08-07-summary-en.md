## [SDSS Releases All-Sky Map of 500,000 Supermassive Black Holes](https://www.sdss.org/black-hole-mapper-release-20/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS) has released Data Release 20 (DR20), featuring an all-sky map of half a million supermassive black holes, along with a companion eROSITA X-ray survey catalog that nearly doubles the number of known X-ray sources to 2 million. This data release significantly expands our understanding of supermassive black holes and their distribution across the universe, providing a valuable resource for astronomers studying galaxy evolution and cosmology. The coordinated optical and X-ray observations enable multi-wavelength studies that were previously impossible at this scale. DR20 includes the first southern hemisphere optical observations from the Black Hole Mapper project, along with multi-epoch tracking of accreting black holes. The eROSITA catalog, based on 1.5 years of operations, covers the second half of the sky and nearly doubles the known X-ray sources to 2 million.

hackernews · MarcoDewey · Aug 7, 15:24 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49211921)

**Background**: Supermassive black holes, which have masses millions to billions times that of the Sun, are found at the centers of most galaxies. The Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS) is a major multi-epoch spectroscopic survey that maps the universe in optical and near-infrared light. The eROSITA X-ray telescope aboard the SRG satellite surveys the sky in X-rays, providing complementary data to optical surveys.
