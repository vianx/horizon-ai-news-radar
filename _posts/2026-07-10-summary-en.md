## [EU preliminarily finds Meta's Instagram, Facebook violate DSA with addictive design](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/home/en) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The European Commission has issued a preliminary finding that Meta's Instagram and Facebook violate the Digital Services Act (DSA) due to their addictive design features, which pose risks to users' physical and mental well-being. This marks a significant enforcement action under the DSA, potentially forcing Meta to redesign core features of its platforms and setting a precedent for regulating addictive algorithms across the tech industry. The Commission's preliminary view focuses on the mismatch between Meta's optimization for 'one more minute' engagement and its reliance on dismissible time-limit popups as mitigation. Meta now has the right to respond before a final decision.

hackernews · jeroenhd · Jul 10, 11:00 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48858292)

**Background**: The Digital Services Act (DSA) is an EU regulation that entered into force in 2022, requiring Very Large Online Platforms (VLOPs) like Instagram and Facebook to conduct risk assessments and implement mitigation measures for systemic risks, including addictive design. The DSA empowers the Commission to impose fines of up to 6% of global annual turnover for non-compliance.
