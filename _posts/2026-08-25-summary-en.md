Apple announced the M6 and M5 Ultra chips on August 25, 2026, marking a significant leap in performance and AI compute. The M6 is Apple's first 2nm chip, featuring up to a 36-core CPU and 80-core GPU, while the M5 Ultra is touted as Apple's most powerful chip ever, with up to 4.3x the peak AI compute performance of the M3 Ultra. This announcement is significant because it underscores Apple's aggressive push into on-device AI, with the M6's Dual 16-core Neural Engine and the M5 Ultra's 32-core Neural Engine. These chips will power next-generation Macs, potentially reshaping the high-performance computing and AI workstation market, and putting pressure on competitors like Intel, AMD, and Qualcomm. ## [FDA Clears First Wearable for Continuous Ketone and Glucose Monitoring](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-authorizes-first-wearable-device-continuously-monitors-both-ketone-levels-and-blood-sugar) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has authorized the Libre Duo 10 Day Continuous Dual Glucose Ketone Monitoring System, the first wearable device that continuously monitors both ketone levels and blood sugar. This clearance applies to people aged 2 years and older living with diabetes. This milestone could significantly improve diabetes management by providing real-time data on both glucose and ketone levels, helping to prevent dangerous conditions like diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA). It may also pave the way for more advanced wearable health technologies and expand access to continuous monitoring for a broader patient population. The device is designed for people with diabetes aged 2 years and older, and it provides continuous measurements of both glucose and ketone levels. While the FDA authorization is a significant step, the device still requires a prescription and may face reimbursement challenges, as noted in community discussions.

hackernews · sunnynagra · Aug 25, 19:07 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49439017)

**Background**: Continuous glucose monitors (CGMs) have been widely used to track blood sugar levels in real time, but ketone monitoring has traditionally relied on fingerstick tests or urine strips. Ketones are produced when the body breaks down fat for energy, and elevated levels can signal a risk of diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA), a serious complication of diabetes. The Libre Duo combines both capabilities into a single wearable device, offering a more comprehensive metabolic picture for patients and clinicians.

## [EVE Online Begins Migration from Python 2.7 to Python 3](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/25/eve-online-move-to-python-3/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

EVE Online has announced the start of its long-awaited migration from Stackless Python 2.7 to Python 3, using the futurize script on 2.4 million lines of code, followed by manual review of approximately 20,000 behavioral differences. This migration is significant because EVE Online is one of the largest and longest-running Python codebases in production, and its successful upgrade will serve as a valuable case study for other organizations still on Python 2. It also highlights the ongoing challenges of maintaining large-scale legacy systems. The migration will use the futurize script to automatically convert code, but manual review is required for the ~20,000 places where Python 2 and 3 behavior differ, such as integer division. The announcement does not specify how they will replace Stackless, but they previously presented a solution using the carbonengine/scheduler library for EVE Frontier.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 25, 22:59

**Background**: EVE Online has run on Stackless Python since its launch in 2003, with the last major upgrade to Stackless Python 2.7 in 2010. Stackless Python is a variant of CPython that provides microthreads (tasklets) for lightweight concurrency, but the project has been discontinued and its GitHub repository archived. Python 2 reached end-of-life in January 2020, making migration to Python 3 necessary for security and maintenance.

A new technical report introduces Thomson, a frontier model trained via continual learning on open-weight models, demonstrating competitive performance across diverse domains. The report argues that this approach makes frontier-level AI achievable for a wide range of institutions with modest compute and personnel budgets. This work challenges the notion that frontier AI development is exclusive to a few well-funded labs, offering a concrete path toward SovereignAI. By democratizing access to frontier capabilities, it could reduce information, economic, and power asymmetries in AI, enabling more organizations to own and govern their AI stacks. Thomson focuses on high-stakes professional domains such as legal, tax, and multilingual tasks, and exhibits a 'π-shaped' performance pattern with broad improvements and minimal forgetting. The approach emphasizes data-centricity and efficiency, making minimal high-impact parameter interventions while preserving plasticity and stability.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Forsaken_Scientist · Aug 25, 10:30

**Background**: Continual learning, also known as lifelong learning, allows models to sequentially learn new tasks while retaining previously acquired knowledge, addressing the common 'catastrophic forgetting' problem. Open-weight models, which release trained parameters publicly, enable others to download, fine-tune, and modify them, forming the basis for such approaches. SovereignAI refers to efforts by organizations or nations to independently build, deploy, and govern AI, reducing dependence on foreign providers.
