## [EU preliminarily finds Instagram, Facebook addictive design violates DSA](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/home/en) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The European Commission has preliminarily found that Meta's Instagram and Facebook violate the Digital Services Act (DSA) due to their addictive design, which exploits minors' vulnerabilities and creates behavioral risks. This marks the first major enforcement action under the DSA targeting addictive design, potentially forcing Meta to redesign core features and setting a precedent for regulating algorithmic amplification across the EU. The preliminary findings focus on Meta's use of personalized feeds and infinite scroll that keep users engaged longer, with the Commission stating that Meta's mitigation measures, such as time-limit popups, are insufficient to address the risks.

hackernews · jeroenhd · Jul 10, 11:00 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48858292)

**Background**: The Digital Services Act (DSA) is an EU regulation that came into force in 2022, requiring large online platforms to assess and mitigate systemic risks, including those related to addictive design and harm to minors. Addictive design refers to interface features like infinite scroll, personalized recommendations, and notifications engineered to maximize user engagement, often at the expense of user well-being.
