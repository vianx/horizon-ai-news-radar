## [Huawei Ascend 950 SuperPoD Debuts, Claims 6.7x Nvidia Compute](https://www.ithome.com/0/978/019.htm) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Huawei publicly demonstrated the Ascend 950 SuperPoD (Atlas 950 SuperPoD) at WAIC 2026, claiming 1 EFLOPS FP8 and 2 EFLOPS FP4 compute power across 1024 Ascend chips, with 256 TB unified memory. According to a China Securities report, its total compute is 6.7 times that of Nvidia's equivalent NVL144 system. This marks a major milestone in AI hardware, potentially shifting the competitive landscape by offering a domestic alternative to Nvidia's high-end systems. If the performance claims hold, it could accelerate AI development in China and reduce reliance on foreign chips. The system uses Huawei's proprietary Lingqu interconnect protocol and supernode architecture, supporting up to 8192 cards without convergence in future configurations. The Ascend 384 SuperPoD has already been commercially deployed in over 750 units across internet, telecom, and finance sectors.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 17, 10:27

**Background**: SuperPoD (Super Power Domain) is a high-density AI computing architecture that scales up many AI chips into a single system using high-speed interconnects, enabling training of large models. Huawei's Lingqu protocol is a proprietary interconnect designed for supernode architectures, similar to Nvidia's NVLink. FP8 and FP4 are low-precision floating-point formats that accelerate AI inference and training with reduced memory usage.

## [First Atmosphere Found on Rocky Exoplanet in Habitable Zone](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy4kdd1e0ejo) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Astronomers have detected an atmosphere around LHS 1140b, a rocky exoplanet orbiting in the habitable zone of a red dwarf star, marking the first such detection for an Earth-like planet. The atmosphere was identified using JWST emission spectroscopy as the planet passed behind its star. This discovery is a milestone in exoplanet science, as it demonstrates that rocky planets around red dwarfs can retain atmospheres despite intense stellar radiation. It also provides a prime target for future telescopes to search for biosignatures, potentially advancing the search for life beyond Earth. The atmosphere is composed primarily of helium, and observations show time-variable escape, with helium detected in 2024 but not in 2025. The planet is about 1.7 times Earth's radius and orbits a red dwarf 49 light-years away, but its 'Earth-like' classification is debated as it may be a mini-Neptune or a water world.

hackernews · neversaydie · Jul 17, 14:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48947560)

**Background**: Red dwarfs are cool, low-mass stars with close-in habitable zones, making planets prone to tidal locking and atmospheric stripping by stellar flares. LHS 1140b is a rocky exoplanet discovered in 2017, and JWST's transit spectroscopy allows astronomers to analyze the chemical composition of exoplanet atmospheres by studying starlight filtered through them.

Friar emphasized that the lowest token price does not equal the lowest task cost, as a more capable model may get the correct answer in one attempt, saving money overall.
