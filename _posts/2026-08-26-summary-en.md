## [FDA Approves First Wearable Monitoring Both Ketones and Glucose](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-authorizes-first-wearable-device-continuously-monitors-both-ketone-levels-and-blood-sugar) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The FDA has authorized the first wearable device in the U.S. that continuously monitors both ketone levels and blood sugar, the Libre Duo 10 Day. This is also the first device in the world to monitor both metrics together in a single device. This authorization could significantly improve diabetes management by providing real-time data on both glucose and ketones, helping to prevent dangerous conditions like diabetic ketoacidosis. It may also pave the way for more integrated wearable health monitors. The device, named Libre Duo 10 Day, is designed for a 10-day wear period. It uses continuous glucose monitoring technology and adds ketone monitoring, which is particularly useful for people with type 1 diabetes who are at risk of ketoacidosis.

hackernews · sunnynagra · Aug 25, 19:07 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49439017)

**Background**: Continuous glucose monitors (CGMs) are wearable sensors that track blood sugar levels in real time, helping people with diabetes manage their condition. Ketones are produced when the body breaks down fat for energy, and high levels can indicate a serious complication called diabetic ketoacidosis. Until now, ketone monitoring required separate devices or fingerstick tests, making it less convenient for patients.

## [OpenAI's Jalapeño Chip Outperforms Nvidia Blackwell in Inference Tests](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/openai-jalapeno-better-than-nvidia) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI has published the first benchmark results for its custom inference chip, Jalapeño, developed with Broadcom. The chip reportedly delivers 1.5 to 1.9 times higher AI workload per watt, 1.7 to 3.6 times lower end-to-end latency, and 2.1 to 4.1 times better performance in high-interaction scenarios compared to Nvidia's GB300 (Blackwell Ultra). This marks a significant milestone in AI hardware, as a major AI lab has developed a chip that challenges Nvidia's dominance in inference. It could lead to more competition, lower costs, and specialized designs tailored for AI workloads, potentially reshaping the AI infrastructure landscape. Jalapeño is a rack-scale architecture similar to Nvidia's NVL72, with 128 accelerators per rack, each capable of 13.4 petaFLOPS at MXFP4. The chip has a rated power of 700W but sustained power consumption is under 550W. It is designed for inference only, not training, and has not been compared to Nvidia's upcoming Vera Rubin.

hackernews · bmulholland · Aug 25, 14:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49434378)

**Background**: AI inference hardware is critical for deploying large language models efficiently. Nvidia's Blackwell architecture has been the industry standard, but custom chips like Jalapeño aim to optimize for specific workloads, reducing data movement and improving utilization. OpenAI plans to deploy Jalapeño in its own data centers by the end of the year, with a second generation in development and a third in design.

## [EVE Online Begins Migration from Stackless Python 2.7 to Python 3](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/25/eve-online-move-to-python-3/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

EVE Online has announced the start of its migration from Stackless Python 2.7 to Python 3, using the futurize script on 2.4 million lines of code and manually reviewing ~20,000 behavioral differences. This migration is significant as it demonstrates a strategy for upgrading a massive, long-running Python codebase, providing insights for other large-scale projects still on Python 2. It also highlights the challenges of moving away from Stackless Python, a discontinued project. The migration involves 2.4 million lines of code and about 20,000 places where Python 2 and 3 behavior differ, such as integer division. The announcement does not specify how Stackless will be replaced, but a previous conference talk described using the carbonengine/scheduler library in EVE Frontier.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 25, 22:59

**Background**: Stackless Python is a variant of Python that provides microthreads (tasklets) for lightweight concurrency, but its GitHub repository was archived in February 2025 and the project is discontinued. The futurize script from the python-future project automates converting Python 2 code to be compatible with Python 3, while manual review is needed for subtle behavioral changes.

## [Continual Learning on Open-Weight Models Enables SovereignAI](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vxvzju/continual_learning_of_frontier_models_for/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A new technical report argues that frontier-level AI performance can be achieved by a wide range of institutions through continual learning on open-weight models, and introduces Thomson, a general-purpose frontier model trained with this approach. The report and open weights are released publicly. This challenges the assumption that only a few heavily funded players can develop frontier models, potentially democratizing AI development and enabling SovereignAI for a wider range of organizations. It could reshape the AI ecosystem by making model ownership and governance more accessible. The continual learning approach uses a modern mid- and post-training stack with safeguards to preserve plasticity and stability, achieving improvements comparable to multiple model generations. Thomson shows a 'π-shaped' performance pattern, improving across many capabilities while nearly eliminating catastrophic forgetting.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Forsaken_Scientist · Aug 25, 10:30

**Background**: Continual learning is an AI approach where a model is sequentially trained on new tasks while retaining previously learned knowledge, addressing the challenge of catastrophic forgetting. Open-weight models make trained parameters publicly available, allowing others to build upon them. SovereignAI refers to an organization's ability to independently build, deploy, and govern AI use, which is often hindered by the high costs and expertise required for frontier model development.

This initiative could pave the way for orbital data centers, potentially offering advantages like global low-latency access and radiative cooling in space. It represents a significant technical challenge and a convergence of AI and aerospace industries, with broad implications for both sectors. SpaceX has not disclosed the specific launch date, orbital altitude, or power and cooling solutions for the system in space.
